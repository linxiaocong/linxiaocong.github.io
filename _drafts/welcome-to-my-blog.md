Welcome! 👋 This is the first post on my new blog.

I built this site with [Jekyll](https://jekyllrb.com/) and the
[Chirpy](https://github.com/cotes2020/jekyll-theme-chirpy) theme. It gives me a
clean, modern layout with dark mode, search, tags, and categories — and the best
part is that writing a new post is as simple as adding a Markdown file.

## What you'll find here

- Notes on software engineering
- Write-ups of side projects
- Occasional thoughts worth remembering

Check out the "How to Write a New Post" guide to see exactly how new posts
